Brand DNA

01
Signal, not noise
Every visit is data. We surface what matters — the tap, the goal, the next move — and leave the rest as ambient context.
02
Real-world warmth
TapIn lives where the physical meets the digital. Layouts breathe. Surfaces feel printed, not painted. Edges are soft (21px radius), corners are kind.
03
Confident clarity
One blue. One ink. One typeface. Hierarchy lives in weight and size — never in chromatic noise. Color enters only to mark state.

Voice — examples

DO
"3 spaces. 12 experiences. 1,402 taps this week."
Lead with the number. Trust the reader. Sentence fragments are fine when they read like a glance at a dashboard.
DON'T
"Welcome back! Your dashboard is ready to provide rich insights…"
No hype. No emoji. No "rich insights." The product is the proof.
