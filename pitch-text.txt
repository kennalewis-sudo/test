Why does TapIn use NFC?
Appendix A
Legacy NFC: Powered, managed device (POS terminal, kiosk). Expensive. Insecure. Immobile.
QR Code: Extremely insecure (scams/fraud), difficult experience, no dynamic capability, does not work
in all environments, and is easily damaged.
TapIn
Inexpensive: the phone IS the reader. Every modern smartphone has a cryptographically secure NFC
reader built in. TapIn’s passive, stateless chip — same as your credit card — works without power on a
device everyone already loves - their own phone.
Secure + Verified: Every tap is cryptographically signed and timestamped server-side. Not a URL
redirect. A verified physical event. This is the foundation of the data moat.
Ubiquitous: Works in any lighting, and difficult to damage
